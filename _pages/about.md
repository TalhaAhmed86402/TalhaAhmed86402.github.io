Bio
---

I am an M.S. student in **Computer Science and Engineering** at [Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU)](https://www.skku.edu/eng/), Suwon, South Korea, and a member of [DASH Lab](https://dash-lab.github.io/) under the supervision of [Prof. Simon S. Woo](https://dash-lab.github.io/Professor). I am a recipient of the **2026 Seoul Tech Scholarship (STS)**. My current research sits at the intersection of **computer vision**, **generative models**, and **AI safety**, with a focus on **deepfake detection** and making generative and vision systems more trustworthy.

Before SKKU, I was a Research Assistant at the [Algorithms in Theory and Practice (ATP) Lab](https://web.lums.edu.pk/~hmd/teaching.html), [Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)](https://lums.edu.pk/), under [Dr. Hassan Mohy-ud-Din](https://web.lums.edu.pk/~hmd/), where I worked on efficient generative models for medical imaging and first-authored [Wave-GMS](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11460419/) (ICASSP 2026). I graduated with **High Distinction** in a joint B.S. in Mathematics–Economics with a minor in Computer Science from LUMS in 2024.
